About Rosie

Here to help you.

Hi, I’m Rosie. Whether through my role as Commercial Director at The Networker UK (opens in a new tab) or in my work as a health insurance specialist, I am truly passionate about helping people.

Healthcare can feel complicated, so I take the time to explain your options clearly—without jargon or scripts. I’ll help you find the right cover tailored to your unique circumstances, whether for yourself, your family, or your business. My support doesn’t end once your plan is set up; I’m always here to guide you through claims or offer advice whenever you need it.

My shift into health insurance was personal. In 2020, a family member was in severe pain on a long waiting list. After opting to pay privately for a knee replacement, they were treated within two weeks. That experience showed me how vital timely care is, which is why I proudly partner exclusively with Vitality Health. Their plans not only help with fast access to approved treatment, but also actively reward you for staying healthy.

Outside of work, staying active is a big part of my own life. When I’m not spending time with my two grown children, Pip and Joe, you’ll find me out walking near my home in the Yorkshire Dales.

If you’d like clear, honest guidance on protecting your health, I’d love to help.
