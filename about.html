About Rosie

I am not a call centre.

My name is Rosie and I am passionate about helping people. I like to take the time to explain health insurance clearly and can advise on what’s best for your circumstances — whether that is for you as an individual, your family or your business, or all of those.

There are no scripts or jargon. Just great customer service. And when you have taken out a plan with me, I go the extra mile to help whenever you need to make a claim, or even if you are just looking for advice. Nothing is too much trouble.

I spent many years running a publishing company. In 2020 a member of my family was in too much pain to keep waiting, paid privately for a knee replacement, and was treated within two weeks. That is why I chose this work, and why I work exclusively with Vitality Health: a full cover promise for approved treatment, and rewards for looking after yourself.

I am a mother of two grown-up children. I live near the Yorkshire Dales, and I still make time to walk.
