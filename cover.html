How the cover works.

Rosie works with Vitality Health because the emphasis is on prevention and early intervention, as well as treatment when you need it. Health insurance is provided by Vitality. My Medical Cover introduces and advises. My Medical Cover is not the insurer.

Levels of cover

Core, and the option to go further.

Members are encouraged to do healthy activities and make healthy choices, through points and statuses — bronze, silver, gold and platinum. There are optional levels of cover, depending on your requirements and budget.

Early intervention

Included in Core Cover

  • Video GP appointments
  • Mental health support — online support and talking therapies
  • Cancer treatment — cancer support through your whole journey
  • Physiotherapy — up to six sessions within Vitality’s Priority Physio network
  • Private hospital treatment, including surgeon and anaesthetist fees
  • Diagnosis — in-patient and day-patient tests and scans
  • Menopause support — dedicated support when you need it

Core cover

  • If you’re admitted to hospital and the treatment is covered by your plan, costs for diagnostic tests and surgeons’ and anaesthetists’ fees will be covered in full
  • Advanced cancer cover offered as part of the plan — all cancer costs covered in full once you have been diagnosed

Comprehensive cover

  • Full outpatient costs — the cost of seeing a consultant, covered in full
  • All diagnostic test costs covered in full

Benefits are subject to any excess that may have been chosen and any exclusions that may apply. Rosie will talk you through what a plan does and does not cover before you decide. A quote is completely no obligation.

Private medical insurance

A plan that pays for private treatment, renewed each year.

Private medical insurance gives you quick access to private facilities and treatments. Its purpose is to help pay for hospital costs you may incur, at a private hospital of your choice. This could be anything from physiotherapy sessions to major heart surgery. It also plays an important role in funding the early diagnosis of conditions you might not even be aware of.

What you are buying is an annually renewable plan to cover you, or your family, or your business colleagues — or a mixture of all.

People often ask about online GP appointments within 48 hours, up to £100 towards private prescriptions and minor diagnostic tests, 24/7 healthcare advice, a fast referral to a specialist, up to six physiotherapy appointments, and countrywide specialists. What you actually receive depends on the plan.

According to BMA Analysis, February 2023, more than 7.2 million people were waiting for treatment. The backlog of procedures was so vast that non-life-threatening operations were having an unprecedented wait.
