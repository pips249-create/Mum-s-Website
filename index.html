Health insurance

What health insurance actually is.

Health insurance, also called private medical insurance, is a plan you renew each year. It helps pay for private treatment. That can mean seeing a specialist, having a test or scan, or being treated in a private hospital, rather than waiting on the NHS.

It does not replace the NHS. It does not cover every illness or every treatment. What you receive depends on the plan, any excess you choose, and any exclusions that apply.

A plan can be for you, your family, your business, or a mix of those. Vitality provides the insurance. Rosie explains what a plan does and doesn’t cover before you decide. A quote is completely no obligation.

The short version

  • You pay for a plan. Vitality is the insurer.
  • The plan helps with the cost of private care. What is included depends on the cover.
  • It can include a GP appointment, physiotherapy, hospital treatment and cancer cover. Not every plan includes all of these.
