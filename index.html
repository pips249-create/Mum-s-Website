Private medical insurance

A plan that pays for private treatment, renewed each year.

Private medical insurance (PMI) gives you quick access to private facilities and treatments. Its purpose is to help pay for hospital costs you may incur, at a private hospital of your choice. This could be anything from physiotherapy sessions to major heart surgery. PMI also plays an important role in funding the early diagnosis of conditions you might not even be aware of.

What you are buying is an annually renewable plan to cover you, or your family, or your business colleagues — or a mixture of all.

NHS waiting lists, February 2023

According to BMA Analysis, February 2023, more than 7.2 million people were waiting for treatment. The backlog of procedures was so vast that non-life-threatening operations were having an unprecedented wait.

Having private medical insurance gives you the ability to bypass these long queues and to be treated much quicker. The 7.2 million figure is the BMA analysis from February 2023, as published on the existing My Medical Cover website.
